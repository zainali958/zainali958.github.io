MedRAG: a medical guidelines assistant

A local assistant that answers questions about public health guidelines in English and Urdu. Every answer cites the document and page it came from, and the assistant says so when the guidelines don't contain an answer.

It runs an open language model on the user's own machine, and retrieval quality is measured against a set of test questions rather than assumed.
